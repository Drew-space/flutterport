Jotta is an offline notes app. Your notes, images and settings stay on your device. We do not run any servers for the app, we do not collect your personal data, and we do not use ads, analytics or tracking.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Jotta ("Jotta", "we", "us") does with your information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and we never receive your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal details.

Things you create or choose inside the app are saved on your own device only. This includes your notes, checklists, tags, images you attach, your display name, your chosen avatar, and your note lock settings.

2. How your data is stored

All of your content is stored locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to us or to any server we control. Because there is no cloud copy, uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently deletes your notes. We cannot recover them for you.

3. Permissions the app may ask for

  • Camera: only if you choose to take a photo to add to a note.
  • Photos and files: only if you choose to attach an existing image to a note.
  • Biometrics or device lock: only if you turn on the optional note lock. The check is done entirely by your device's operating system. Your fingerprint, face data or PIN are never seen by the app or sent anywhere.

All permissions are optional. You can use the app without granting them, and you can remove them at any time in your device settings.

4. Internet connections

Jotta works fully offline, and your notes and images never leave your device. When your device is online, two small features may make direct requests to third-party services:

  • Profile avatars: generated avatars may be requested from the DiceBear avatar service. The request contains the avatar style and a seed value used to draw the picture, which may be based on the display name you choose.
  • Fonts: the app may download its text font from Google Fonts.

These requests go straight from your device to those services. Like any web request, they may expose your IP address and basic device information to them. They never include your notes or images. Those services have their own privacy policies.

5. Third parties, ads and analytics

The app does not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking services. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. The only third-party services involved are the two described in section 4.

6. Data retention and deletion

We hold none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete. You can delete any note inside the app at any time, and you can remove everything by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in your device settings.

7. Security

Your content stays on your device, so its safety depends on your device's own security. We recommend using a screen lock, and the optional note lock for sensitive notes.

8. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Since the app collects no personal data at all, no child's data is collected either.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Please check back occasionally.
