Delete Your Account

This page explains how to delete your account and data for Soul Mate, developed by Enudeme Chukwuka.

Soul Mate lets you sign up with your email and password, or with your Google account. The steps below work for both.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the Soul Mate app and sign in with the email or Google account you used to sign up.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when asked.

Your account and data are deleted from our database as soon as you confirm.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, you can ask us to delete your account.

  1. Send an email to chukwukaenudeme@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the same email address you used to sign up (or the Google account you signed in with).
  3. We will delete your account and confirm by email within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details (name, email address, profile information)
  • Your sign-in record
  • Your chat messages and conversation history
  • Any memories the app saved about you
  • Your voice notes
  • Your diamond balance and any subscription status in the app

What is kept

Nothing is kept once your account is deleted. We do not keep a copy of your data after deletion.

Google account note

If you signed in with Google, deleting your Soul Mate account removes your data from our app only. It does not delete your Google account.
