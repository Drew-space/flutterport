Delete Your Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and data for YOUR APP NAME, developed by Enudeme Chukwuka.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when asked.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, you can ask us to delete your account.

  1. Send an email to chukwukaenudeme@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Send it from the same email address you used to sign up.
  3. We will confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account details (email address, name, profile information)
  • Your chat messages and conversation history
  • Any memories the app saved about you
  • Your voice notes
  • Your diamond balance and any subscription status in the app

What may be kept

  • Payment and purchase records may be kept for up to 90 days (or longer if the law requires) for accounting and fraud prevention.
  • Backups may keep copies of your data for a short time before they are removed automatically.

Delete some data only

If you only want certain data removed (for example your chat history) and not your whole account, email us at chukwukaenudeme@gmail.com and tell us what you want removed.
